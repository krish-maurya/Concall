# Kaynes Technology India Limited — Q2 FY26 (2026-11-16)
*Source: NSE · https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/KAYNESTECH_20112026152655_TranscriptoftheEarningAnnouncement.pdf · filed 2026-11-20*

## TL;DR
- Q2 FY27 consolidated revenue INR9,525 million, EBITDA INR1,256 million (13.2% margin), PAT INR398 million.
- H1 FY27 revenue INR18,987 million, EBITDA INR2,681 million (14.1% margin).
- Order book INR105,635 million.
- Kaynes Semicon Unit 2 and Kaynes Circuits operational with customer approvals.
- Acquisition of Toyokawa JV expands into thin‑film transistor display modules.

## Management commentary
- Kaynes is moving up the value chain from PCBA to a design and product company.
- Focus on balance sheet strength and cash discipline while investing for long‑term growth.
- Strategic growth engines: Kaynes Semicon and Kaynes Circuits.
- Entry into aerospace, railways, space, and medical electronics.
- Partnership with Vishay is a long‑term strategic arrangement.

## Notable Q&A
**Q (not stated):** What is the status of Kaynes Semicon Unit 2 and Kaynes Circuits?

**A:** Both are now operational with multiple products receiving customer approvals.

**Q (not stated):** How does the Toyokawa JV fit into Kaynes strategy?

**A:** It gives entry into thin‑film transistor display modules, complementing existing PCB assemblies for automotive displays.

**Q (Achal Lohade with Nuvama Institutional Equities):** What is the upside for the metering business?

**A:** The change in auditor has come and then reconfirmed that the way what we have accounted is right, and it has given an upside for us in terms of the metering business to the level of INR40 crores-INR50 crores.

**Q (Mayank Pandey, Emkay Global Financial Services Limited):** Will the Vishay investment be used towards ISM 1.0 commitments or ISM 2.0?

**A:** The investment will go back into the parent company and will ease the cash situation at the parent company. For ISM 2.0, we will invest further at the partnership level when appropriate.
