The problem statement they are facing, vehicle inward, material inspection, and accounts payable were run through a manual disconnected process, and the SAP access was limited due to licensing costs.
The solution we have implemented was a fully integrated digital, and the entire accounts payable workflow sitting as a layer integrated with their SAP ERP. We have deployed three agents which cover document extraction, purchase order matching, and an auto-categorization agent. When we think of it, the same line item, which could be a raw material, ancillary cost, packaging, et cetera, sits on many open purchase orders at once.
When this line item appears on every new invoice, the entire matching and categorization to the specific open purchase order happens within seconds, helping in a quick turnaround post the material inspection. The impact of this  is that the conglomerate  is seeing faster vehicle turnaround, revenue leakage control through credit note automation, and a centralized access control dashboard where information is available to relevant stakeholders without having to pay for an additional SAP license.
The next example I would like to give is, I want to talk about our engagement with a large EPC
company, which manages an ecosystem of more than 10,000 vendors, most of which are classified as MSMEs, and processes around 40,000 invoices a year across  its different manufacturing and project sites. The problem statement they are facing is as follows.
One, the entire vendor onboarding was manual and took more than four days to onboard a single small vendor. Two, invoice processing ran on a manual email-driven workflow that needed more than 15 resources to reconcile and process. Three, procurement, invoicing, and payments sat on different systems, not only making it harder to manage operationally, but also making it hard to enforce statutory compliance for tax and MSME payments.
The solution we have implemented was our core accounts payable stack, which has helped in automating these workflows. We are also deploying our AI-driven RFX module, consisting of multiple pre-set templates and questionnaire builders, along with a weighted multi-attribute auction mechanism. Here, we are providing copilots for vendor onboarding, RFX drafting, negotiation, and collusion detection, along with agents for document verifications and statutory validations. The impact is as follows.
One, vendor onboarding fell from four days to 15 minutes, with 14 statutory validations being automated. Two, entire invoice processing is now AI-powered, not only for data extraction, but even three-way matching. The entire load is now managed by just three people, where earlier 15
people were used, freeing up significant bandwidth and resource cost.
The digitization of the process has helped them get near real-time visibility into their working capital, and  instances  of  duplicate payments, delayed MSME payments have reduced considerably.
