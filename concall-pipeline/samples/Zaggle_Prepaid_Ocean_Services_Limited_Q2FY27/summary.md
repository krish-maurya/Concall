# Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services Limited — Q2 FY27 (2026-11-13)
*Source: NSE · https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/ZAGGLE_20112026180931_SE_Intimation_-_Earning_Call_transcript.pdf · filed 2026-11-20*

## TL;DR
- Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services reported Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 34% and first‑time H1 FY27 revenue exceeding INR1,000 crores.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin on a consolidated basis was about 7.7% in Q2 FY27 and is expected to remain around 7%-8% going forward.
- Positive operating cash flow was achieved at both standalone and consolidated levels in Q2 FY27.
- Dice acquisition contributed SaaS revenue in Q2 FY27, with 65%-70% of contracts novated.
- Mobileware EBITDA last quarter was due to one‑time events; GreenEdge revenue grew but margins may not grow sequentially.
- Program fee growth slowed to around 10% due to partner bank invoicing; cash back kept at about 66%; revenue growth Q‑on‑Q 22%, Y‑on‑Y 12.5%.
- Zagg Money had about 3 million transactions month‑on‑month; processed INR 3 lakh crore worth of transactions in H1.
- Uncertain incremental revenue from MDR implementation.

## Management commentary
- Revenue growth driven by loyalty and reward segment and festive season.
- Payroll costs increased due to Dice acquisition but offset by novated contract revenue.
- Operating cash flow improved through better invoicing, payment terms, and customer advances.
- Implemented AI‑driven accounts payable stack to automate vendor onboarding, reducing onboarding time from four days to 15 minutes.
- Mobileware EBITDA last quarter was due to one‑time events.
- GreenEdge revenue grew but margins may not grow sequentially.
- Investments made to ensure security for Mobileware UPI switch and GreenEdge NPCI redemptions.
- Program fee growth slowed to around 10% due to partner bank invoicing.
- Cash back kept at about 66% and revenue growth Q‑on‑Q 22%, Y‑on‑Y 12.5%.
- Expect momentum between this quarter and next quarter.
- Zagg Money had about 3 million transactions month‑on‑month.
- Processed INR 3 lakh crore worth of transactions in H1.
- Uncertain incremental revenue from MDR implementation.

## Notable Q&A
**Q (Siva from iThought PMS):** How much of our Q2 revenue is from Dice, and what would be the cost base for them as well?

**A:** Dice revenue is entirely in the SaaS income, and about 65%-70% of those contracts have novated.

**Q (not stated):** Will UPI on MDR have a positive impact on our business and the fintech industry?

**A:** Yes, it will generate revenue from the huge volume of transactions.

**Q (Siva from iThought PMS):** On the program fee, the growth has slowed down to around 10%, right? When can we expect this growth to go back to 20%-30% rate?

**A:** We see momentum kicking in this quarter and hopefully between this quarter and next quarter.

**Q (Siva from iThought PMS):** How much of the GTV is currently from UPI, and how much would be eligible for MDR?

**A:** Right now, the GTV does not include the UPI transactions.

**Q (Siva from iThought PMS):** What is the incremental revenue if MDR is implemented?

**A:** Difficult to quantify at this moment because we also don't exactly know the quantum of MDR that the government will bring in.
