# Belrise Industries Limited — Q2 FY27 (2026-11-20)
*Source: NSE · https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/BELRISE_20112026112442_BELRISE_Intimation_of_Transcript_of_earnings_call_20112026.pdf · filed 2026-11-20*

## TL;DR
- Belrise Industries reported its strongest quarter ever in Q2 FY27 with PAT of INR1,663 million and revenue of INR27,464 million, up 17% YoY.
- New Dharwad and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar facilities will ramp up in Q1 FY28, expected to generate INR1,500–2,000 million and INR1,000–1,200 million annual revenue respectively.
- Suspension business gained traction in Q2 FY27 with six OEM customers onboarded and ROCE improved to 15.3%.
- Aerospace and defense target to contribute upwards of 10% of revenues in the medium‑term, with manufacturing revenue from the segment expected to exceed 10% in the next 12–18 months.
- Merger with Badve auto component business expected to complete in Q4 FY27 or Q1 FY28.
- Trading business margins under pressure but cost pressures easing.
- No revision to mid‑teens revenue growth guidance.
- Aerospace, renewables, and electronics seen as future growth drivers.
- Aerospace revenue contribution estimated at 2.5–3.5% of manufacturing revenue.

## Management commentary
- Trading business margins are seeing a bit of pressure.
- Cost pressures seem to be largely behind us, expecting return to normalcy over next few quarters.
- No revision in guidance; maintain mid‑teens revenue growth with stable EBITDA margins.
- Aerospace, renewables, and electronics will be growth drivers over and above automotive.

## Guidance / outlook
- Consolidated Q4 FY27: mid‑teens revenue growth guidance, stable EBITDA margins.
