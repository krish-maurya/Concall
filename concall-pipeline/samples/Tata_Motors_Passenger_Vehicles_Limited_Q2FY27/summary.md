# Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited — Q2 FY27 (19-Nov-202)
*Source: NSE · https://nsearchives.nseindia.in/corporate/TATAMOTORSSJS_19112026202307_NSEBSETranscriptQ2FY27.pdf*

## TL;DR
- Q2 FY27 saw strong launch momentum with Tata.CARS brand and Aeris sedan in India, and three new luxury EVs at JLR.
- Consolidated revenue ~Rs. 97,000 Cr, up 34% YoY; EBITDA margin 6.2%, EBIT margin 1.1%; PBT before exceptional items ~Rs. 400 Cr.
- Exceptional charges of Rs. 6,700 Cr recorded at JLR.
- Consolidated net debt closed at ~Rs. 51,000 Cr.
- India business revenue hit Rs. 20,000 Cr, up 50% YoY, with EV market share 43% and 23% penetration.

## Management commentary
- Launch of Tata.CARS brand and Aeris sedan drove best‑ever offtake and retail month in October 2026.
- JLR launched three new luxury electric vehicles with deliveries expected from early 2027.
- Alternate powertrains now account for more than half of volumes.
- Commodity pressures expected to remain elevated in Q3 with further increases in steel, rubber, and copper.
- Strong portfolio launches drive growth; capacity expansion underway.
- Will capitalize on greener powertrains and scale production capacity.
- Strengthening profitability through cost reduction, PLI approvals, and pricing actions.
- We will keep increasing price progressively where headroom exists.
- We are confident we can offset PLI benefits with price and cost strategies.

## Guidance / outlook
- FY27 EBIT 2–3% range.
- FY27 cash outflow ~GBP 1.25bn.
- FY28 double‑digit EBITDA and cash generation.
- FY30 return to net cash.

## Risks / concerns
- Commodity price pressures
- Supply constraints in select value chain areas
- Adverse commodity pressures likely to remain in Q3
- China market continues to decline at alarming speed
- MENA demand and logistics struggle due to conflict

## Segment performance
- **Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles**: Launch of Aeris sedan and Tata.CARS brand drove record offtakes.
- **Jaguar Land Rover**: Three new luxury EVs launched; recovery from cyber incident.
- **India business**: Revenue Rs. 20,000 Cr, 50% YoY growth; 67,000 monthly offtakes, 14.3% market share.
- **EVs**: Highest quarterly volumes, 16,000 monthly offtakes, 43% market share, 90% YoY growth.
- **Defender**: Sales up >20% YoY; UK wholesales strong, China and MENA declining.

## Notable Q&A
**Q (Investor):** How are commodity pressures expected to evolve?

**A:** Commodity pressures remain elevated in Q3 with further increases in steel, rubber, and copper, but are expected to plateau in Q4.

**Q (Analyst):** What is the plan for Capex?

**A:** H1 Capex was Rs. 2,700 Cr; we expect higher spending over the next 18 to 24 months for capacity builds.

**Q (Kapil):** What percentage of bookings are EVs?

**A:** Roughly 30% of bookings are EV bookings, 40–45% of pending bookings are EVs.

**Q (Investor):** What is the rough price hike this year?

**A:** About 1.7% across ICE and EV models.

**Q (Investor):** What benefit will we get in FY27/FY28?

**A:** £1.7bn split across ex‑works, warranty, and cost efficiencies.

**Q (Investor):** What is the restructuring cost?

**A:** £530m this quarter, £295m from voluntary redundancy.


## Management tone: cautious
